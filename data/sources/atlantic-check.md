## Steps Jacob must do personally for ONC (lead 4)
1. Open https://data.oceannetworks.ca/Registration and create a free Oceans 3.0 account (verify email).
2. Log in, open Profile, tab "Web Services API", copy the token.
3. In Data Search / Data Archive, choose location Holyrood (Conception Bay), device category video camera, and check the licence shown on the data product and download a couple of days of clips. Or use the Python `onc` client (token in env var ONC_TOKEN): getLocations, getDeviceCategories, then getArchivefileByLocation.
4. Check each file's licence before it goes in a shipped model. Do not put the token in a repo.
Caveat: 85 m deep, clearer-than-harbour water, snow crab, so it is unlike Coastal Action's turbid shallow green-crab footage; useful only as a weak fixed-camera sanity check.
