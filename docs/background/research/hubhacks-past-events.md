Research date: Thu 5 Nov 2026. Tags: [fetched] = page opened; [snippet] = search result only; [inference] = my reasoning.

## Bottom line
Very little is public. Only the 2025 winner is confirmed from a fetched page. No 2023 event turned up. The 2024 and 2025 Saint Mary's claims rest on social posts I could not open. No judge names, judging criteria, or 2026 partner names are public.

## Past events

**2025 (Halifax)**
- Challenge: a sustainable solution to improve local food security. Over 70 Nova Scotia post-secondary students, three days. [fetched] https://www.nscc.ca/about/news/stories/2025/nscc-wins-rbc-hubhacks-2025.asp
- Winner: NSCC Team 2, FoodSafe NS, a proof-of-concept web app. It helps users find food banks and filter stock by dietary restriction. Food banks would scan donations to update inventory. Prize $2,500. [fetched] same URL
- RBC's Rachel MacLeod said judges were impressed by the "innovation, professionalism and creativity" of every team. [fetched] same URL
- Disputed: a Saint Mary's team posted on LinkedIn that they were "#Champions" of HubHacks 2025 with "Connecta", an AI mobility platform for affordable Halifax transit. [snippet] Source is a personal post; no official announcement found. The two wins are not reconciled.

**2024 (Halifax, first edition per one source)**

## Patterns in winners
- The only fetched winner (FoodSafe NS) was a working end-user tool with a clear user (food bank clients and staff), a data flow, and a proof of concept. [fetched] One example is not a pattern. [inference]
- The 2025 theme was a community problem with a local, measurable outcome. The 2026 brief's framing (discovery, source links, partner ownership; fish ID with video) fits the same shape. [inference]
- Judges' stated values are generic: innovation, professionalism, creativity. [fetched] Nothing more specific is public.
